The machine watches a streamer's games, detects the real plays from official match data, edits vertical clips with AI, runs computer-vision quality gates so nothing broken ever ships, and publishes to the streamer's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok — on autopilot. One recent night it mined old VODs, edited and published 23 pieces while I slept.

I operate the whole company solo by directing a fleet of AI coding agents that share a git-based brain: they write the code, audit each other and hand off work across machines. **Most of the green squares below are that fleet, working.**
